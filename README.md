### 👋 Hi there

I'm Lensa. I studied software engineering at Addis Ababa University, then spent
two and a half years at **A2SV** building **AKIL**, Ethiopia's first
volunteer–NGO matching platform. I picked up how to lead a team there, and how
to actually build and ship a product to users. It went from an empty repo to
1,400+ volunteers and 35 partner organisations.

In September 2025 I moved to Madrid for a master's at **IE** on a full
scholarship. My thesis focused on **Kolmogorov–Arnold Networks**. Liu et al.
(2024) credited KAN's performance to its edge-based design; I proved you can
construct a B-spline MLP that computes the same function. It was the splines
all along. I then used KAN on quantum detector tomography and got stable POVMs
out of almost no data.

Now I'm building agentic systems and looking for software engineering roles
across EMEA.
